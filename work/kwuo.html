Why it exists

Most language apps treat Igbo as an afterthought, if they include it at all. Kwuo starts from the other end: short rounds that make you recognise, say and use real words, so practice feels like play rather than homework.

I designed and built the game flows, the interface, and the content tools behind it.

Four ways to play

Each game trains a different part of speaking Igbo.

vocabularyIgbo Words MatcherMatch Igbo words to their meanings before the clock runs out.
party gameIgbo Heads UpHold the word to your forehead while friends describe it in Igbo.
one wordOfu OkwuChoose one word that best describes an image.
riddlesGwam Gwam GwamThe classic “tell me, tell me” riddle game, rebuilt for the screen.

Fun facts from building it

the name

“Kwuo” is Igbo for “speak”. The whole product is one long nudge to say the words out loud.

the rebuild

Igbo Heads Up got a full rebuild — new state, new components, while this very portfolio was being designed.

no deploys

Game content is edited from an admin I built, so new words go live without touching code.
