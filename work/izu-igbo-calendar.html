the idea

Traditional igbo week

The traditional Igbo week has four market days — Eke, Orie, Afọ and Nkwọ, and they still shape when markets meet, when ceremonies happen, and which day people say they were born on.

EKEORIEAFỌNKWỌ
the web app

App features

Shows today’s date, the day of the week, the market day and the moon phase side by side. Month and year views let you plan ahead.

the android app

In your pocket

The Android app brings the same calendar to your phone to track market days and find your Igbo birthday anywhere. It’s built with React Native and Expo, and it’s live on Google Play.
