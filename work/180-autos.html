type

Font Family

A tall, condensed display face for the headlines, a clean sans for everything you read, and one pixel font saved for the footer.

Bebas Neuedisplay — headlines, prices, section titles. Always uppercase.
Albert Sanstext — specs, forms and everything you read.
Press Start 2Ppixel — only in the footer, to match the car.
colour

Green means go

The signal green comes straight from the logo, and it’s kept for actions like booking a test drive, viewing inventory. Everything else is ink, paper and a few greys, like tarmac and road paint.
